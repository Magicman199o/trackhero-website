01 / Why Track Hero

Most tools tell you what happened. Track Hero tells you what it cost, and how to fix it.

01

Analytics and errors, finally connected.

Most teams run one tool for user behaviour and another for crashes, and never join the two. Track Hero ties every error to the people it hit and the clicks that led there.

So yousee that a bug cost you 23 checkouts, not just that it happened.
02

One line. No tracking plan.

Paste one script tag and pageviews, clicks, sessions, devices and countries start flowing. No event spreadsheet, no week of setup, no engineer required.

So youhave real data from your very next visitor, not next quarter.
03

Fixes, not just alerts.

Every error comes with a plain-English summary and a ready-made prompt: the error, the stack trace and the steps that triggered it. Paste it into your AI coding tool or hand it to a teammate.

So yougo from "something broke" to a fix in minutes.
04

Every visitor becomes a person.

When someone signs up, their earlier anonymous visits are joined to their account automatically. You see their whole story, from first click to last session.

So youknow which pages and sources bring the customers who stay.
