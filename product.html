01 / Calibration

One line. Everything else is automatic.

Paste it into the head of your app. Pageviews, clicks, sessions and frontend errors start flowing without a tracking plan.

<script src="https://track-hero.vercel.app/trackhero.js" data-key="pk_your_key" defer></script>
~3.4 KB

Compressed size of the script. Loads after your page, so it never slows the first paint.

pk_ key

A public key that can only send data, never read it. Safe in browser code.

consent mode

Hold tracking until a visitor agrees, then switch it on with one call.
