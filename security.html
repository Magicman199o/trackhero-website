Your users trust you with their data. You can trust us with it.

Track Hero is built to learn how people use your product without learning who they are, unless you choose to tell it. Here is exactly what that means.

01 / Our promise

Collect less. Protect everything. Hide nothing.

Behaviour, not identities.

We record what happens in your app: pages, clicks, sessions and errors. Not what people type, and not who they are, unless you identify them yourself.

Scrubbed before it's stored.

Emails, card numbers, passwords and tokens are stripped from addresses, errors and event details the moment they arrive, before anything reaches the database.

You stay in control.

You decide which sites can send data, when tracking starts, and when a project and its data are deleted. Every switch is yours.

02 / What we collect

A clear line between what we keep and what we never touch.

What Track Hero records

  • Pages visited and buttons clickedWith the button's label, so you know what was clicked.
  • Sessions and time on siteUsing a random ID, not a name.
  • Browser, device and countryThe country is worked out on arrival. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • Errors and the steps before themThe error message, where it happened and the last few clicks.
  • Details you choose to sendYour own events, and user traits if you call identify().

What it never records

  • Anything typed into formsThe script never reads inputs, text areas or dropdowns.
  • Passwords and payment detailsPassword and card fields are ignored entirely.
  • IP addressesUsed only for a moment to stop abuse, then discarded.
  • Secrets in URLsTokens, keys and codes in links are replaced before storage.
  • Anything you mark privateAdd data-th-ignore to any part of your page and clicks inside it are skipped.

04 / Your controls

Safeguards built in, switched on by default.

Separate public and secret keysThe public key in your website can only send data, never read it. The secret key for your server is shown once, stored scrambled, and can be replaced any time.always on
Allowed domainsOnly accept data from the sites you list, so nobody can fill your project with noise using your public key.per project
Consent modeCollect nothing until a visitor agrees, then start with one call. You can also give visitors an opt-out switch.one setting
Encrypted in transitEvery request to Track Hero travels over HTTPS.always on
Private by accountEvery page checks that you own the project before showing a single number. Database access from the browser is blocked entirely.always on
Abuse limitsSize limits and rate limits on incoming data keep a broken or hostile script from flooding your project.automatic
Delete a projectRemove a project and everything it collected from your settings.self-serve
